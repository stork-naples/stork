Microwave Installation in Naples and SW Florida

Over-the-range and built-in microwaves have to be mounted, vented, and wired correctly - not just set on a counter - and Stork Home Services handles that work in Naples, Marco Island, Bonita Springs, and South Estero.

We confirm the cabinet or wall opening, run recirculating or outdoor venting, and make a clean electrical connection so the unit sits square over the cooktop.

Replacement or a new hood-microwave combo, we remove the old unit, protect the range below, and test heat, turntable, and fan before we leave.
