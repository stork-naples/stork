Garbage Disposal Installation in Naples and SW Florida

A garbage disposal is a plumbing and electrical job under the sink, not a quick twist-on - Stork Home Services installs disposals throughout Naples, Marco Island, Bonita Springs, and South Estero.

We fit the sink flange, wire the switch or air switch, tie in the dishwasher drain when needed, and test for leaks at the flange and trap.

New install or a replacement for a jammed or leaking unit, we match the kitchen, haul the old disposal, and leave the cabinet dry and working.
