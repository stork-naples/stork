Freezer Installation in Naples and SW Florida

Chest and upright freezers need a dedicated, level spot and enough airflow to keep up with Southwest Florida heat - Stork Home Services installs freezers throughout Naples, Marco Island, Bonita Springs, and South Estero.

We verify the circuit, set the unit plumb, and confirm door or lid seals so frozen food stays protected in a garage, pantry, or kitchen addition.

New install or a swap for a failed freezer, we deliver, position, and test the temperature so you are not guessing whether the unit is pulling down.
