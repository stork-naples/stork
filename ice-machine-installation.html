Ice Machine Installation in Naples and SW Florida

In SW Florida’s warm climate, a working ice machine isn’t a luxury - it’s a necessity. Stork Home Services installs residential ice machines throughout Naples, Marco Island, Bonita Springs, and South Estero.

We connect water supply and drain, set filtration where the unit needs it, and understand ice maker mechanics from refrigeration cycles to built-in, undercounter, and freestanding cabinets.

Whether you are adding ice to a wet bar or replacing a tired machine, we level the unit, run the first harvest, and confirm reliable ice production before we pack up.
